# Overview

**PPB-OTR-UVC** is an autonomous field robotic platform designed for **precision UV-based disease management in specialty crops**. The robot integrates a UV treatment system with dual-GPS-based autonomous navigation, enabling accurate and repeatable operation in vineyard and other specialty-crop environments. By following a predefined **prescription map** (which can be generated by [PPBv2](https://yiyuanlinxx.github.io/robots/ppbv2) or [PPB-NG](https://yiyuanlinxx.github.io/robots/ppbng)), PPB-OTR-UVC automatically activates and deactivates its UV lamps at designated treatment locations, allowing UV treatment to be selectively applied only where disease management is needed.

The platform supports **variable and dynamic travel speed control** to balance treatment efficacy and operational efficiency. Within designated treatment zones, PPB-OTR-UVC automatically reduces its travel speed to ensure that the target **UV dose** is delivered to the crop canopy. The treatment speed can be determined according to the required UV dose and the configuration and output characteristics of the UV lamp system. After leaving a treatment zone, the robot automatically returns to a higher travel speed, reducing unnecessary operation time in non-treatment areas and improving overall field efficiency.

Through the integration of autonomous navigation, prescription-map-based treatment control, and adaptive speed management, PPB-OTR-UVC provides a scalable platform for **site-specific and automated disease management**, reducing unnecessary UV exposure while maintaining the required treatment dose in targeted areas.

**Key Features:**

* UV treatment module for automated crop disease management
* Dual-GPS system for autonomous navigation and accurate field positioning
* Prescription-map-based automatic UV lamp activation and deactivation
* Variable and dynamic speed control for maintaining the required UV dose within treatment zones
* High-speed travel in non-treatment zones to improve overall operational efficiency
* Site-specific treatment capability for precision disease management

The platform is designed for autonomous outdoor operation and can support precision disease-management workflows in vineyards and other specialty-crop production systems. By combining spatial disease information with automated UV treatment, PPB-OTR-UVC enables **targeted, repeatable, and efficient field-scale disease intervention**.
